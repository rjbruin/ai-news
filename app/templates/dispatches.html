How does your own Dispatch work?

Your own Dispatch is a custom AI-written publication tuned to your interests — an AI agent reads news sources and writes an edition on a schedule you choose (daily or weekly), instead of you having to read everything yourself.

It starts out cloned from the System Dispatch's settings, so you have something working right away, and you can then tune its interests, content style, and sources to your own taste.

Running it takes AI model usage, which costs real money — you bring your own OpenRouter API key to pay for it, rather than this app charging a subscription fee. You can add your key any time on the Your Dispatch page after setting up.

You can also add your own news sources (newsletters, RSS feeds), and publish your Dispatch so other people can follow and read it too.
